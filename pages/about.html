About
Daily T Studio

A small independent design studio making colourful, hand-drawn work — wall art, activity designs and app illustrations.

The studio

Everything is
made by hand

Daily T Studio is a small independent design studio run by someone who has loved making things for as long as she can remember — the aspiring artist in the photo is proof of that (circa 2000).

Every illustration, pattern and character is drawn and built from scratch — no templates, no shortcuts. The work is rooted in a love of colour, storytelling and the small details that make a space feel like home.

The style sits somewhere between Scandinavian restraint and joyful colour — never too serious, never too loud.

What guides
the work

✦
Made by hand

Every design starts with a pencil, a brush or a stylus — drawn from imagination, not assembled from stock.

🎨
Colour with intention

Colour is never an afterthought — it's chosen carefully to create atmosphere, warmth and a sense of calm joy.

♻️
Print on demand

Prints are produced only when ordered — no overstock, no waste. Delivered via Gelato's global network.
