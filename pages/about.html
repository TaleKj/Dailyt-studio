About
Daily T Studio

Everything is
made by hand

Daily T Studio is a small independent design studio. Every illustration, pattern and character is drawn, painted and built from scratch — no templates, no shortcuts.

The work is rooted in a love of colour, storytelling and the small details that make a space feel like home. Whether it's an abstract print for a living room wall or a playful activity poster for a child's bedroom, the intention is the same: to make something that genuinely delights.

The style sits somewhere between Scandinavian restraint and joyful colour — never too serious, never too loud.

What guides
the work

✦
Made by hand

Every design starts with a pencil, a brush or a stylus — drawn from imagination, not assembled from stock.

🎨
Colour with intention

Colour is never an afterthought — it's chosen carefully to create atmosphere, warmth and a sense of calm joy.

♻️
Print on demand

Prints are produced only when ordered — no overstock, no waste. Delivered via Gelato's global network.

The process

From idea
to your wall

Each design usually starts with a loose idea — a colour palette, a feeling, a scene. It's sketched, refined and built up in layers until it feels right. There's no fixed formula, just a lot of iteration and the occasional cup of tea.
