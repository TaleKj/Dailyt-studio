DIY Physical print

Advent

Two art prints — a Christmas backdrop and a sheet of 24 characters, one for each day of advent. The first image shows them together as they could be displayed; the second and third show each print separately. You get both as physical prints, produced in high quality and available up to 70×100 cm. What you make of them is entirely up to you — hang the backdrop, cut out the 24 characters and use them as an advent calendar, or let your imagination take you somewhere else entirely. The design came out of a search for a simple, affordable and reusable advent calendar for a two-year-old — and not finding one anywhere.
