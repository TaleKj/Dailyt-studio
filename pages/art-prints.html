Silken Currents

Soft, flowing forms in muted pastels — Silken Currents is inspired by water and rivers, capturing that sense of calm movement you find by the water's edge. Started as an acrylic painting on canvas, then redrawn digitally by hand using an Apple Pencil in Linearity Curve.
