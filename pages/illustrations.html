A case study

Konka — Illustrations

Konka started as a simple idea — a sports tracking app that we actually wanted to use ourselves. What began as a hobby project grew into a fully launched product on the App Store.

My role covers the visual identity: the logo and character, icon set and colour palette. The playful, hand-drawn aesthetic was a deliberate choice — sport should feel joyful, not clinical.
