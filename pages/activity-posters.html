Advent

Two art posters — a Christmas backdrop and a sheet of 24 characters, one for each day of advent. The first image shows them together as they could be displayed; the second and third show each poster separately. You get both as PDF files, printable in high quality up to at least 70×100 cm. What you make of them is entirely up to you — hang the backdrop, cut out the 24 characters and use them as an advent calendar, or let your imagination take you somewhere else entirely. The design came out of a search for a simple, affordable and reusable advent calendar for a two-year-old — and not finding one anywhere.
