Konka —
App Design

Visual identity, logo and icon design for Konka — a sports tracking app available now on the App Store. Built together with my partner, currently on version 1 with version 2 in development.

Download on the App Store
The project

A side project
built from scratch

Konka started as a simple idea — a sports tracking app that we actually wanted to use ourselves. What began as a hobby project grew into a fully launched product on the App Store.

My role covers the entire visual identity: the logo and character, icon set, colour palette and the overall look and feel of the interface. The playful, hand-drawn aesthetic was a deliberate choice — sport should feel joyful, not clinical.

Version 2 is currently in development, bringing new features and a refined experience.

Screens

Inside the
app

The app tracks training sessions, visualises progress over time and shows routes on a map. The visual language is consistent throughout — soft colours, hand-drawn icons and a friendly, approachable tone.
